The first thing I touch every morning is an ASP.NET time tracking web app. 

I have done it for 10 years. 5 minutes eight times a day, or forgot and reconstruct yesterday from memory. Or get overwhelmed and backfill 3 months. 

I resented the most. This week I decided that was the last time.

I pointed Claude Code armed with a headless browser. Two hours later I had the backend mapped out and wrapped in an MCP server. Now Claude drafts my timesheet from Slack, GitHub, Granola, and the calendar — context it already has — and the entries are more accurate than what I used to type.

Every week, I'm going to pick the dumbest manual task I still do, and delete it.

What's yours?
